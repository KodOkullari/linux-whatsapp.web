# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Harassment,
insults, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing others'
private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions
that violate this code. Report abuse privately to the repository owner through
their GitHub profile. All complaints will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.
